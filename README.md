# Opportunity Tracker

A platform designed to help students **track internships, job opportunities, and important deadlines** without searching through hundreds of WhatsApp messages, emails, or forwarded notifications.

---

## 🚨 Problem Statement

An internship opportunity appears in a WhatsApp group. Someone forwards it to another group, and then it arrives again by email.

Now students are scrolling through **300+ messages** trying to find:

- Opportunity link  
- Company name  
- Role  
- Eligibility  
- Application deadline  

### Question

**How might we ensure students can track opportunities and deadlines without searching through endless message threads?**

---

## 💡 Solution

This project converts **unstructured opportunity notifications** into **organized and searchable opportunities**.

The system collects scattered information and presents it in a clear format so students can easily see:

- Company name  
- Role  
- Eligibility  
- Application link  
- Deadline  

This helps students **quickly find opportunities without wasting time scrolling through messages.**

---

## ✨ Features

- Organized opportunity listings  
- Easy-to-read opportunity details  
- Deadline tracking  
- Searchable opportunities  
- Simple and user-friendly interface
